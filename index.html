About the app

Solaryn is a mobile app that provides climate- and sun-related information for locations you choose or enter. It is designed to use offline caching where possible.

What data is processed?

Data stored on your device

  • Saved locations, app settings, and cached analysis results may be stored on your device (local database and preference stores).
  • This data is not automatically sent to our servers (the app has no backend account of its own).

Location

  • If you use features such as “Use my location”, your device location is used within the limits allowed by the OS to show location-based analysis and mark the map.
  • If you do not use location or deny permission, you can still run analysis by entering coordinates or selecting a point on the map.

Internet and third-party services

When online, requests to fetch climate and calculation data for the selected location may be sent directly from your device over HTTPS to public scientific / map services such as the following (often only coordinates or query parameters):

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (address search / reverse geocoding)
  • Open-Meteo and related endpoints
  • European Commission PVGIS (JRC) solar horizon service
  • NASA POWER climate normals API
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap data queries)

Those providers have their own privacy policies; use of their services is subject to their terms.

Accounts and identity

If Solaryn does not offer in-app sign-up / sign-in, no account-related personal data is processed for that purpose.

Children

The app is not designed specifically for children. Parental guidance is recommended.
